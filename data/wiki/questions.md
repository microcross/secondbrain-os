# Open Questions

> Persistent, deduped list of unresolved questions surfaced from daily canvas, meetings, and Slack. Daily organize re-surfaces still-open questions in the daily organized doc until they get closed.
>
> **How to use:** When a question is answered, move it to the Answered section at the bottom and fill in the answer. Don't delete — the record is useful when the same question resurfaces later. Status values: `Open`, `Answered`, `Dismissed`.
